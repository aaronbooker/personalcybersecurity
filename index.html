About Aaron

Decades in technology, explained in plain English.

I’m a skilled generalist. I’ve spent my career in technology, and I like nothing better than helping people get comfortable with tools that look intimidating from the outside.

I teach Computer Information Systems at Whatcom Community College, including the college’s first AI course, and I was awarded a National Community College Cybersecurity Fellowship funded by the National Science Foundation.

Through my experience running my own small businesses, including Varvid, and supporting small businesses with business-focused IT through Hardlines, I’ve learned how businesses actually run, what owners and team leaders juggle every day, and where technology helps or gets in the way.

At Varvid I also led sales and marketing, and our clients, including Nike, Adobe and Intuit, mostly streamed live for marketing. Nearly 17 years of working alongside them gave me a close look at how companies like these use marketing and social media effectively.

Today I bring that experience to small businesses, nonprofits and community groups across Whatcom, Skagit, San Juan and Island counties.
